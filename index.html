How it works

A simple workflow designed for production lines. Capture → analyze → decide → act.

1

Capture

Industrial cameras capture images (and where needed, multi-angle / 3D views) on the moving line.

2

Analyze

Edge compute runs vision pipelines to detect defects under strict latency constraints.

3

Decide

Each unit is classified as fit/not-fit with confidence and defect category for traceability.

4

Act

Integration enables rejection/marking workflows and quality reporting aligned to factory SOPs.
